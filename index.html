The Sixth Drawer

There was supposed to be something mysterious in here. A curse, perhaps. A questionable antique. Maybe a tiny ghost with terrible manners.

Instead, there's just something I wanted you to know.

Halloween was incredible because I got to spend it with you. But my favorite part wasn't the holiday, or the decorations, or even the spooky stuff we both love.

It was simply getting to exist beside you.

And the really wonderful thing? We don't need Halloween as an excuse to keep being our wonderfully strange selves together.

So here's to whatever odd, beautiful, unexpected things November brings. I'm really happy you're part of my days.
