The Ghosts I Never Buried

An invitation into the quieter corners of me
Before the doors open

Tomorrow is Halloween, so tonight I thought I'd introduce you to a few of my ghosts.

Not the kind hiding under beds or lurking in dark hallways.

The kind that follow you quietly through life. The things you learn to live with, the things you rarely talk about, and the parts of yourself you get so good at hiding that eventually people forget they're even there.

I'm not sharing these because I need sympathy. And certainly not because I expect you to fix anything.

I just think that if I'm going to let someone into my life, really let them in, they deserve to know there's more to me than the guy who makes jokes, takes care of things, and always tries to make everything okay.

So tonight, I'm leaving a few doors unlocked. 🖤

Three doors. Three things rarely said aloud.
Open them in any order. There are no questions to answer.

The final door · The man behind the ghosts
❤️ 🖤

And then there's you.

I want to be careful with how I say this, because I never want you to feel like you've been handed the responsibility of healing things you didn't break.

You haven't.

But I do want you to know something.

When I make you smile, when I plan something ridiculous just to brighten your day, when I want to hold you a little longer or kiss you one more time, none of that comes from trying to earn my place in your life.

It's because I genuinely enjoy loving people in the little ways that matter. And with you, those things feel wonderfully natural.

I'm not looking for someone to rescue me from my past.

I'm looking forward to building something that isn't defined by it.

And I think that's why I'm telling you all of this.

Because I don't want to just show you the polished, funny, capable parts of me.

I want to be comfortable enough to show you the unfinished parts, too.

You don't have to chase away my ghosts, Nell.

I just wanted you to know they're there.

❤️ 🖤

Thank you for wandering through the darker corners of my world. You don't have to say anything. I'm just glad you're here.

No answers needed. No ghosts to fix. Just a little more of me.
