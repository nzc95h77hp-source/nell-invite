Nell,

I know some days feel heavier than others. And I know there isn't always a reason you can point to, or something someone can say that makes it better.

I'm not going to pretend I have the magic words.

But I can remind you of something.

You don't have to be happy tonight. You don't have to be entertaining, productive, or even particularly talkative.

You can just be you.

That's already someone I love spending time with.

❤️

✦   Three quiet thoughts, whenever you're ready   ✦
You don't owe the world every ounce of your energy. Some nights, getting through the day is enough. Everything else can wait.

You have this way of making ordinary moments feel like something worth remembering.

A conversation, a laugh, a kiss, or simply being close to you.

I don't think you realize how much those little moments mean to me.

And I hope, even on the days when it's difficult to see anything particularly wonderful about yourself, you know there's someone who sees it in you without even having to look.

❤️

You don't have to earn my affection by having a good day. I'm still going to want to see you, hear your voice, and steal another kiss whenever you'll let me. ❤️
✦

I wish I could make every difficult day easier for you.

I can't.

But I can be someone who doesn't make those days harder.

Someone who listens when you want to talk, sits quietly when you don't, and never expects you to pretend you're okay for my benefit.

And for what it's worth, Nell...

I'm really glad you're here.

Even on the hard days.

Especially when you need a little extra gentleness.

🖤❤️
